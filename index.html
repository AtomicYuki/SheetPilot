Questions

Do you upload my file?

No. Your CSV is opened and cleaned inside your browser. We only store your email and plan status.

Do I need an account?

Not for the free plan. You only sign in, with a code sent to your email, when you want Premium.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. You review changes first, and you can undo before you download.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Payments go through Lemon Squeezy. Cancel anytime and keep Premium until your paid period ends.

What file types work?

CSV files and Excel (.xlsx) files. For Excel files, SheetPilot uses the first sheet.
