Questions

Do you upload my file?

No. Your CSV is opened and cleaned inside your browser. We only store your email and plan status, so Premium works on any computer.

Do I need an account?

Not for the free plan. You only sign in, with a code sent to your email, when you want Premium.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. You review changes first, and you can undo before you download.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Payments go through Lemon Squeezy. You can cancel anytime and keep Premium until your paid period ends.

What file types work?

CSV files. Excel files can be saved as CSV first from Excel or Google Sheets.
